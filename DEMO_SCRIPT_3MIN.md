# FinWise Bharat Voice · Three-minute demo

The walkthrough uses browser captures from the synthetic FinWise workspace. Timbre reads the narration. The Hindi sample is the same audio that Prisma transcribed during the test. The screenshot clips show different points in the same test workspace as expenses were added.

## 00:00 to 00:16 · From speech to spending

**Visual** — Open with the Bharat Voice page in white and Finance Chat in black. Show the theme controls.

**Narration**

> FinWise Bharat Voice helps people record an everyday expense in the language they speak. Both white and black modes are available from the voice page and the workspace. The recording leads to a budget check, a spoken confirmation, and a saved ledger entry.

## 00:16 to 00:29 · Record an expense

**Visual** — Show the expense upload and review. Play the Hindi sample after the narration.

**Narration**

> In the test workspace, I upload a short Hindi recording of a tea purchase. Here is the sample that was sent to Prisma.

## 00:29 to 00:49 · Check the projected budget

**Visual** — Show the browser review with ₹50, Food & Dining, and ₹735 remaining.

**Narration**

> Prisma transcribes the recording. FinWise extracts fifty rupees and suggests Food and Dining. The review shows seven hundred thirty five rupees left if I save this draft. I can correct the text or discard the draft before it changes my ledger.

## 00:49 to 01:08 · Save to the ledger

**Visual** — Show the Transactions view in INR after two separate ₹50 test saves.

**Narration**

> After saving, Timbre reads the confirmation aloud. The transaction view shows both sample expenses in rupees, with a total of one hundred rupees. The amount and category are available for later review in the same ledger.

## 01:08 to 01:26 · Compare months in chat

**Visual** — Show the ledger conversation. The initial typed question establishes the spending topic. The imported clip says “And last month?” and the trace shows zero model calls.

**Narration**

> I ask how much I spent this month. FinWise shows one dollar and twenty cents in the workspace currency. Then I ask, and last month? The same chat checks October. It finds no recorded expenses. Both answers come from the ledger.

## 01:26 to 01:44 · Hear an answer in Telugu

**Visual** — Show the built-in browser voice Q&A test. The test used a preset Telugu question.

**Narration**

> In voice question and answer mode, I try a Telugu question about this month’s expenses. The app summarizes the two saved purchases, names Food and Dining as the highest category, and uses Timbre to read the answer aloud.

## 01:44 to 02:04 · Dictate a draft and check the math

**Visual** — Show the compound-interest question appended to the existing composer draft. Then show the calculator result and zero-model-call trace.

**Narration**

> Dictation adds the spoken question to my existing draft. I can edit it before sending.
>
> At ten percent, one thousand rupees grows to one thousand two hundred ten rupees in two years. The calculator verifies two hundred ten rupees in total interest.

## 02:04 to 02:22 · Hands-free conversation and follow-ups

**Visual** — First show the checked Hands-free conversation control and the ₹1,210 reply. Then show an earlier turn in the same workspace where the user asked “What about after three years?” and the conversation returned ₹1,331.

**Narration**

> I turn on hands-free conversation and import a short question. Prisma transcribes it, the calculator checks the math, and Timbre reads the reply.
>
> In the earlier follow-up, FinWise kept the same principal and annual rate and returned one thousand three hundred and thirty-one rupees after three years.

## 02:22 to 02:41 · Take a checklist with you

**Visual** — Show the PDF created in chat and the Download PDF and Open in Files controls.

**Narration**

> I can also ask FinWise to turn a short checklist into a PDF. The document card lets me download it, open it in Files, or ask a follow up question about it. The checklist is created directly from the text I supplied.
