OUR APPROACH: WHY THIS MULTI-AGENT PRODUCT MODEL FEELS STRONGER TO USE

1. The Core Product Choice
This project does not treat AI as a decorative chatbot bolted onto a finance UI. The architecture combines operational product features with a dedicated AI workflow. That means users do not interact with an isolated prompt box; they interact with a system that already understands auth, org context, financial records, workflows, and automation state.

2. Why The Multi-Agent Layer Helps
The AI Core uses a master router and specialist financial agents instead of asking one model to do everything. This is useful because personal finance questions usually combine several domains at once. A good answer may need to look at spending behavior, budget constraints, debt pressure, and investment tradeoffs before returning a single recommendation.

3. Why It Builds More Trust
The repository exposes workflow metadata such as provider choice, agents involved, and workflow trace information. That makes the system feel more inspectable than a black-box assistant. In finance, that matters. Users are more likely to trust a guided process when they can see that the system followed a structured path instead of jumping straight to a conclusion.

4. Why The Local UX Is Better Now
The local build improves satisfaction in several practical ways:
- the chat experience is more polished and easier to navigate
- the register and verify-email screens are clearer and more product-like
- development verification is faster because the OTP is surfaced directly
- local origin mismatches no longer break the auth flow

5. Product Outcome
The result is a portfolio app that feels more complete. It does not only answer finance questions; it gives users a workspace where AI, data, collaboration, and automation all reinforce each other.
