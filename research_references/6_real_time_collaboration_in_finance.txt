REAL-TIME COLLABORATION AND ITS IMPACT ON FINANCIAL PLANNING

1. The Shift to Multi-Tenant Finance
Most traditional personal finance literature assumes a single independent user. However, sociological studies reveal that personal finance is frequently a household endeavor. Our platform's transition to a multi-tenant "Organization" model directly addresses this. By enabling Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and shared ledgers, the platform maps to real-world sociological financial structures rather than forcing users into individual silos.

2. Asynchronous and Synchronous Planning
Features like threaded annotations on individual transactions and a live Activity Feed reduce Information Asymmetry between partners or financial advisors. Research suggests that high transparency and clear communication tools directly correlate with higher goal-achievement rates in household finance, validating our inclusion of dedicated social collaboration features within a financial tracker.
