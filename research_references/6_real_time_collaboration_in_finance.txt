REAL-TIME COLLABORATION AND SHARED FINANCIAL OPERATIONS

1. Collaboration Is A Product Requirement
Finance decisions often involve more than one person. The repository acknowledges this by building collaboration around organizations rather than assuming a single isolated user.

2. Collaboration Features In The Repo
The server and data model layers include support for:
- organizations and org members
- invite flows
- activity feeds
- comments
- notifications
- shared workflow execution
- share-link style distribution paths

3. Why This Matters
Shared financial planning needs more than read-only access. People need context, review loops, and visible actions. Activity feeds and notifications make system behavior easier to follow, while comments create a place for discussion inside the finance workflow instead of outside it.

4. Connection To Automation
The workflow engine and autopilot flows extend collaboration further. Financial work can be scheduled, reviewed, approved, and executed in structured stages. That turns the application into a small finance operations workspace, not just a private tracker.

5. Portfolio Relevance
This collaboration layer increases the project’s credibility. It shows that the application has been designed with realistic product behavior in mind, including the messy reality that financial planning is often shared, reviewed, and negotiated.
