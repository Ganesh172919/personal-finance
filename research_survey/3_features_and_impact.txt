SURVEY PART 3: FEATURES, CAPABILITIES, AND PRODUCT IMPACT

1. User-Facing Feature Areas
The project includes several major product surfaces:
- Authentication and onboarding
- Financial dashboard and analytics
- AI chat and analysis
- Workflow automation and autopilot
- Organizations and collaboration
- Receipts, exports, journals, and supporting content

2. Onboarding And Access
Users can register, verify email, log in, and retrieve a profile. The local build now keeps the full verification flow intact while exposing the OTP in development for smoother demos and testing.

3. Finance Workspace
The application includes a financial dashboard and multiple server-side finance models for transactions, budgets, recurring rules, month close data, categories, goals, and usage tracking. This makes it more than a chat demo; it is a structured finance product with persistence and operational state.

4. AI Product Surface
The AI experience is one of the strongest portfolio elements in the repository:
- A dedicated chat experience
- AI status and provider visibility
- Multi-agent routing based on financial intent
- Workflow trace metadata for transparency
- Tool call support and synthesis output

This design lets the app explain not only the answer, but also the route taken to produce it.

5. Automation And Operational Features
The workflow engine supports:
- manual runs
- cron-based scheduling
- event-triggered automation
- task creation, notifications, and exports

Autopilot extends this idea into a controlled finance workflow with plan, simulate, approve, and execute stages.

6. Content And Storytelling
The repository also contains blog, growth story, and media-oriented features. These help position the application as a richer product platform rather than a single-screen financial calculator.

7. Product Impact
From a portfolio perspective, the project demonstrates:
- full-stack application structure
- AI orchestration beyond simple prompt wrappers
- real auth and org-aware backend behavior
- extensibility through plugins and templates
- enough UI depth to feel like a product, not just a prototype
