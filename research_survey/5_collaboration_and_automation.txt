SURVEY PART 5: COLLABORATION AND AUTOMATION WORKFLOWS

1. Real-Time Collaboration
Finance is often a shared responsibility. The platform supports multiple users on a single organization profile:
- Activity Feed: A real-time stream mapping all enterprise actions and system events.
- Comments and Annotations: Users can thread comments on specific transactions, goals, and budget items to foster discussion.
- Notification Center: Push-like alerts using Server-Sent Events (SSE) keep users instantly updated.

2. Automation Engine
- Workflow Triggers: Event-driven architecture that triggers actions based on domain criteria (e.g., categorizing certain merchants automatically).
- Autopilot: AI-generated financial plans follow a "simulate -> approve -> execute" pipeline, ensuring the AI cannot make unauthorized financial movements without human oversight.
